FieldView.Live ("FieldView") is operated by Noctusoft, Inc. ("Noctusoft", "we", "us").
Payments for FieldView run through the **Noctusoft Store** (store.noctusoft.com), the
checkout and payout service Noctusoft operates for its apps, with **Stripe** as the payment
processor. By accepting this agreement and setting up payouts, you (the "Recipient", "you")
agree to the following:

1. **You are the seller.** When a viewer buys access to one of your streams or games, the
   charge is made directly on **your own Stripe account**, a connected account that Noctusoft
   opens for you on Stripe when you set up payouts. You are the merchant for the sale.
   Noctusoft never holds your money; it receives only the platform fee described in section 3.

2. **Your Stripe account.** To receive payouts you complete Stripe's onboarding (identity,
   business details and a bank account) and accept the
   [Stripe Connected Account Agreement](https://stripe.com/connect-account/legal), which
   governs your relationship with Stripe. Your account has no separate Stripe login; you
   reach it through FieldView. Keep your information accurate and your account in good
   standing. Stripe may ask you for more information at any time and may pause charges or
   payouts until you provide it.

3. **Platform fee.** Noctusoft keeps a platform fee of **10% of each purchase price**,
   collected by Stripe as an application fee at the moment of the charge. We may change the
   fee for future sales with notice through FieldView.

4. **Processing fees.** Stripe's processing fees, at Stripe's then-current rates for your
   account, are deducted from your account on each charge, in addition to the platform fee.
   For illustration, at Stripe's rates when this version was written, a $1.00 sale leaves
   about $0.57 to you after a $0.10 platform fee and about $0.33 in Stripe fees.

5. **Payouts.** Stripe pays your available balance to your bank account on Stripe's payout
   schedule for your account (currently daily, after Stripe's settlement delay; the first
   payout to a new account can take longer). Noctusoft does not control payout timing and
   does not handle your money on its way to your bank.

6. **Refunds, disputes and negative balances.** Noctusoft can refund a sale, in full or in
   part, at your request or where FieldView's viewer terms require it (for example when a
   stream never aired). When a sale is refunded, the platform fee on the refunded amount is
   returned to you; Stripe's processing fee is not. Chargebacks and disputes on your sales,
   and any dispute fees, are charged to your account under Stripe's terms. If your account
   balance goes negative, Stripe may recover the amount from your later sales or your bank
   account, and you remain responsible for it.

7. **Taxes and records.** You are responsible for all taxes on your earnings. Where the law
   requires, Stripe or Noctusoft may collect tax information from you and issue tax forms
   (for example IRS Form 1099-K). FieldView keeps a record of each sale, fee and refund on
   your account.

8. **Viewers.** Viewers pay on a checkout page hosted by Stripe for the Noctusoft Store and
   receive an emailed receipt. Their purchases are also governed by FieldView's Terms of
   Service.

9. **Content and conduct.** You are responsible for the streams you sell: that you hold the
   rights to broadcast and sell them (including league, venue and broadcast rights, and any
   consents needed for minors who appear), and that they comply with the law and FieldView's
   Terms of Service. Noctusoft may suspend sales or access, and may refund affected
   purchases, for violations.

10. **Ending the arrangement.** You may stop selling at any time and may ask us to
    disconnect your payout account; Noctusoft may also end this arrangement with notice.
    Ending it stops new charges through FieldView. Balances you have already earned still pay
    out under Stripe's schedule, less any refunds, disputes or negative balances, and your
    responsibilities for past sales continue.

11. **Changes to this agreement.** This agreement is versioned. When it changes, FieldView
    asks you to accept the new version before you change your payout setup. We record the
    version you accepted, when you accepted it and the network address it was accepted from.

12. **No warranty; limitation of liability.** FieldView and the Noctusoft Store are provided
